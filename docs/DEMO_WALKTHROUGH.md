# Demonstration Walkthrough

This walkthrough is intended for a portfolio demonstration using synthetic records.

## Suggested sequence

1. **Overview** — Introduce the platform and the prototype governance workflow.
2. **Register AI system / AI inventory** — Show how an AI use case and its accountable owners are recorded.
3. **System details** — Explain the system context, intended purpose, users, data categories, and deployment information.
4. **Risk assessment** — Demonstrate the six assessment dimensions and the need for human review.
5. **Control Management** — Show the starter control library, indicative framework references, system-level implementation/effectiveness assessment, evidence references, and remediation actions.
6. **AI Assurance & Evaluation** — Explain how test cases define the question, expected behaviour, source, and severity.
7. **Evaluation Execution** — Run or review an evaluation and show recorded observations and scores.
8. **AI Security Assurance** — Explain the security-oriented scenarios and be explicit about what the prototype can and cannot test.
9. **Assurance Findings & Remediation** — Show how a result can be linked to a finding and assigned an owner and due date.
10. **Executive AI Governance Dashboard** — Close with the consolidated risk, controls, assurance, findings, evidence, and remediation metrics.

## Example narrative

"This prototype applies a quality-engineering and risk-based assurance approach to AI governance. It connects AI inventory, risk assessment, control tracking, evaluation evidence, findings, and executive reporting in one workspace. The purpose is to demonstrate an auditable workflow and human oversight—not to claim that a framework assessment or regulatory certification has been completed."

## Demonstration hygiene

- Use fictional systems, names, policies, and records.
- Do not show employee data, confidential organisational content, credentials, or personal information.
- Explain that evaluation outcomes are observations from defined test scenarios, not proof of universal safety or security.
- Distinguish a control being assessed from the control being effective.
